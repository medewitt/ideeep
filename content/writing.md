# Scientific & Policy Writing

Science is not finished until it is communicated, and it is not useful until someone can act on it.
A result that cannot be explained, a hypothesis that cannot be stated sharply, or a finding that never reaches the decision it bears on will not advance the field or the response.

This hub collects short, reusable references on the craft of writing, each pairing a skeleton with a worked infectious-disease example.
The pages are meant to be assigned one at a time across courses, the same way the [Quantitative Methods](math.md) pages are.
Two longer prose overviews anchor the collection: [scientific writing](scientific-writing.md) covers the arc from a first question to a finished manuscript, and [scientific pathways](scientific-pathways.md) covers the research career and the funding system.
The snippet pages below break that material into assignable pieces and add the half of communication that academic training usually leaves out — writing for the people who make decisions.

Writing is thinking made visible.
It rewards practice and repetition as much as any statistical or laboratory skill.
