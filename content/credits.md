## Illustrations

Scientific illustrations are from the [NIH BioArt Source](https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/)
(NIAID) and are in the **public domain**, courtesy of NIAID. Individual
illustrations were created by NIAID staff (e.g., Ryan Kissinger). These include
the depictions of viruses and antibodies, laboratory instruments (qPCR machine,
liquid-chromatography instrument), a blood smear, a Western blot, a microplate,
a syringe, and a tick.
